Privacy

fonts.gnass.buzz is a private, non-commercial hobby project. There are no ads, nothing is for sale, and there is no business behind it.

Who is responsible

Felix Gnass · E-Mail

What this site does not do

  • No cookies.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads.
  • No accounts and no forms.
  • No scripts or other content from third-party servers. The only exception is Google Fonts, see below. Links to other sites (for example GitHub) load nothing until you click them.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., USA. When you open a page, your browser sends the usual request data to GitHub's servers, including your IP address. GitHub keeps this data in server logs to run and protect the service. I have no access to these logs. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in delivering the site reliably (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). For details, see GitHub's privacy statement.

Google Fonts

This site is a browser for open-source fonts, most of them from Google Fonts. To show you these fonts, the app loads them from Google Fonts, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. The app does this only after you click “Start exploring” on its start page, which tells you about it. Before that click, no request goes to Google. The app's own interface fonts and the fonts of the independent foundries come from GitHub Pages, like the rest of the site.

When the app loads a font from Google, your browser connects to fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. Google then receives your IP address, your browser's user agent, the address of the app (as referrer), and the font families and characters that the app asks for. Google does not set cookies for these requests. Google can process this data in the USA; Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

The legal basis is your consent when you start the app (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR) and my legitimate interest in showing you the fonts that you look for, which the app cannot do without loading them (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). You can withdraw your consent at any time: close the app and do not start it again. For details, see the Google Fonts privacy FAQ and Google's privacy policy.

The app

The app runs in your browser. The letters that you draw and the text that you type are processed on your device. They are never uploaded to me or to any server; only the characters of your preview text go to Google as part of the font requests (see above). The app keeps your shortlist of fonts in your browser's local storage; this data stays on your device, and you can delete it in your browser settings.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object. Write me an e-mail for this. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Hamburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.
